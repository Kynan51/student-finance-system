Everything in one calm dashboard

Irregular income, handled

Record HELB disbursements, money from parents or guardians, bursaries and side-hustle payments as they arrive.

Expenses in seconds

Add an expense manually or paste an M-Pesa confirmation SMS. The app reads the amount and date and suggests a category.

Budgets that warn early

Set monthly or semester limits per category, add fixed obligations like hostel rent, and get warned before you overspend.

Virtual savings goals

Set money aside on paper for rent, fees or emergencies. Your available balance shows only what is truly free to spend.

Honest AI insights

See your estimated financial runway, possible shortfalls, unusual spending and trends, each with the numbers behind it.

Reports and install

Download monthly or semester reports as CSV or PDF, and install the site on your phone like an app.

Your data, your control

  • No M-Pesa PINs, bank passwords or phone numbers are ever requested.
  • No connection to M-Pesa or bank APIs: you enter or paste your own records.
  • Every record is visible only to you, and you can delete your account and data at any time.
